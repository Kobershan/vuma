# Stage 10c — Quotes, Invoices & Sales Analytics

## Status

**DONE (2026-09-07)** · Depends on: **10, 14, 08c** · Reference reading: `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` (sales documents), `docs/DECISIONS.md` ADR-074, ADR-102, ADR-112

## Objective

The documentary and analytical output of the trading system. Core sales (Stage 10) handles pricing and promotions; Order Management (Stage 14) handles allocation; Cross-Company Availability (Stage 08c) handles the reservation ledger. This stage exists to turn those operational facts into the legally binding documents a business hands to a customer or auditor, and to aggregate them for management reporting. Getting the document shape wrong breaks tax compliance and B2B integrations; getting the multi-company split wrong breaks the trading group's accounts.

## Deliverables

### Quotes
- **Generation:** Convert a cart or a basket into a non-binding quote. Captures prices, taxes, and pack sizes at the moment of creation (snapshotting, not live resolution).
- **Lifecycle:** Draft, Issued, Accepted, Rejected, Expired.
- **Conversion:** An accepted quote converts into an Order (Stage 14) or a direct Sale (Stage 09) at the till. If a quote must hold stock, it explicitly invokes Stage 08c's reservation ledger via a `QuoteHold` intent, but by default a quote is a promise of price, not a promise of stock.
- **Delivery:** Print at the till, email as a PDF attachment (via Stage 05 documents), or expose via the public API for B2B storefronts.
- **Expiry:** Automated expiration workflow with customer reminders before the validity period lapses.

### Invoices
- **Generation from Fulfilment:** An invoice is generated when an order is fulfilled or a sale is completed.
- **Multi-Company Splitting (ADR-102):** When a single order is sourced and fulfilled across multiple companies in a trading group (via Stage 08c), the system **must generate one distinct invoice per company**. Each invoice contains only the lines, taxes, and totals attributable to that specific company's database. Group-level consolidated invoices are read-only projections generated by Stage 06d's group read models, never the source of truth.
- **Pack Size Representation (ADR-112):** Every invoice line must carry and print the **pack size** (e.g., "2 x Case of 12", "5 x Each") alongside the base unit quantity and price. This is resolved from Stage 10's price lists at the time of invoice generation and snapshotted immutably.
- **Immutability & Correction:** Once an invoice is finalized and posted to the Stage 07 financial ledger, it is immutable. Corrections must be processed via Credit Notes (Stage 10), never by mutating the invoice aggregate.
- **Delivery & Archiving:** PDF generation, email delivery, and long-term archival linked to the customer account.

### Sales Analytics
- **Company-Scoped Read Models:** Daily, weekly, monthly, and year-to-date aggregations per company, product category, and sales channel.
- **Group-Level Projections:** Consolidated analytics across a trading group. These are derived from stale read models (ADR-119) via the Stage 06d group read model machinery. They are for planning and trend analysis only; they never drive real-time commit or document generation decisions.
- **Dimensions:** Margin analysis (revenue minus Stage 08 cost of sale), tax liability summaries, and promotion effectiveness (tying back to Stage 10 campaigns).

## Business rules

- **Documents are immutable snapshots.** Once an invoice is issued, its tax amounts, pack sizes, and prices are locked. Subsequent changes to the master data (Stage 06) or price lists (Stage 10) do not retroactively alter issued documents.
- **One order, N invoices (ADR-102).** A trading group order does not produce a single fiscal document. It produces one fiscal document per company whose stock was consumed. Cross-company allocation is an operational fact; fiscal liability remains strictly within the consuming company's database.
- **Pack size is a legal document requirement (ADR-112).** B2B and wholesale customers require pack sizes for receiving and inventory. The invoice line must explicitly state the unit of measure and pack configuration as it existed at the time of sale.
- **Analytics never block trade.** Sales analytics read models may be stale by design (ADR-119). A delay in group-level aggregation must never cause a timeout or failure at the till or in the order fulfilment pipeline.
- **Reconciliation:** Total invoiced amounts per company must continuously reconcile to the Stage 07 AR sub-ledger and the Stage 09 POS shift closures.

## Tests / acceptance

- **Quote lifecycle:** Draft → Issued → Accepted → Converted to Order, with prices snapshotting correctly against a mid-process Stage 10 price list change.
- **Multi-company invoice split:** A Stage 14 order fulfilled 60% by Company A (in "Cases of 10") and 40% by Company B (in "Eachs") via Stage 08c produces **exactly two** distinct invoices in their respective databases.
- **Pack size rendering:** The generated PDF and API response for the split invoices correctly display the snapshotted pack size descriptions (ADR-112).
- **Immutability:** Attempting to mutate an issued invoice fails architecture and unit tests; corrections must route through the Stage 10 Credit Note flow.
- **Liability reconciliation:** Total of all issued invoices per company equals the Stage 07 AR control account balance after 10,000 seeded transactions.
- **Stale group analytics:** A group-level analytics query returns successfully even when the Stage 06d saga coordinator is lagging behind the company databases (proving ADR-119 compliance).
- **Permission test:** A user with `sales.invoice.view` for Company A cannot view Company B's invoices, even if both belong to the same trading group.

## Implementation Requirements

- Domain entities follow the `[Replicated]` + `IImmutableRecord` patterns.
- Commands carry `[CommandSideEffect(SideEffect.Write)]`; queries carry none.
- Every command handler uses exactly one `ICompanyDbContextFactory` (MultiCompanyGuardTests).
- All money uses `Money` value object; all quantities use `Quantity`.
- New migrations are reversible (`Down` tested).
- Coverage ≥ 80% on Domain + Application for new code.
- Endpoints appear in `/openapi/v1.json` with examples and error responses.
- Permissions registered in the catalogue; module manifest updated.

## Data/Database Impact

New tables in each company database:
- `sales.quotes` — quote header with lifecycle state, expiry, customer reference
- `sales.quote_lines` — line items with snapshotted price/tax/pack size
- `sales.invoices` — invoice header, immutable once issued
- `sales.invoice_lines` — line items with pack size snapshot
- `sales.analytics` — company-scoped daily/weekly/monthly/year-to-date rollups
- `registry.analytics_projections` — group-level consolidated read model (keyed `(tenant_id, company_id, period)`)

New migrations for both company and registry contexts. Reversible.

## API Impact

New endpoint groups:
- `POST /api/v1/quotes` — create a quote from a basket
- `GET /api/v1/quotes/{id}` — read a quote
- `POST /api/v1/quotes/{id}/issue` — issue a quote
- `POST /api/v1/quotes/{id}/accept` — accept a quote (converts to order)
- `POST /api/v1/quotes/{id}/reject` — reject a quote
- `POST /api/v1/quotes/{id}/expire` — expire a quote
- `GET /api/v1/quotes` — list quotes with filters
- `POST /api/v1/invoices` — generate invoice(s) from a fulfilled order/sale
- `GET /api/v1/invoices/{id}` — read an invoice
- `GET /api/v1/invoices` — list invoices with company scoping
- `GET /api/v1/analytics/sales` — company-scoped sales analytics
- `GET /api/v1/analytics/sales/group` — group-level analytics (stale by design)

## Security

- New permissions: `sales.quote.manage`, `sales.quote.view`, `sales.invoice.manage`, `sales.invoice.view`, `sales.analytics.view`.
- Company scoping enforced at the endpoint layer and in every query handler.
- A user with permission for Company A cannot view Company B's invoices, even if both belong to the same trading group (permission test).

## Sync/Offline Impact

- Quotes and invoices replicate `StoreToCloud`/`Bidirectional` as appropriate.
- Analytics read models are `NodeLocal` (rebuilt per node like `StockBalance`).
- Group analytics are fed by each company's outbox via the registry projection machinery.
